# SipSense — User Guide

SipSense is a whiskey discovery app that helps you explore, learn about, and find whiskeys you'll love. It has a database of 10,000–25,000 bottles and uses a recommendation engine + AI chat to guide you.

---

## Getting Started — Pick a Username

Many features (ratings, favorites, recommendations) are tied to a username stored in your browser. There's no registration form — just type a username the first time you're asked on any page and it will be saved automatically.

You can enter it on the **Browse**, **Whiskey Detail**, or **For You** pages. Once set, it persists across sessions in your browser.

---

## Pages & Features

### Browse (`/`)

The main whiskey discovery page. Shows a grid of bottles from the database.

**What you can do:**
- **Search** — type any name or distillery in the search bar. Results update as you type.
- **Filter by category** — bourbon, scotch, irish, japanese, rye, canadian
- **Filter by region** — Speyside, Islay, Kentucky, etc.
- **Filter by flavor** — find all whiskeys tagged with a specific note (e.g. "vanilla", "peaty")
- **Set a price range** — enter a min and/or max price in USD
- **Sort** — by rating (default), price low/high, age, or alphabetically
- **Active filter chips** — see all active filters at the top; click the × on any chip to remove it, or "Clear all"

Each result shows a whiskey card with name, distillery, category, ABV, age, region, price, and star rating. Click any card to go to the bottle's detail page.

---

### Whiskey Detail (`/whiskey/:id`)

The full profile page for a single bottle.

**What you'll see:**
- Name, distillery, all metadata (category, region, age, ABV, price) as badges
- **"What makes this special"** — a 2–3 sentence plain-English description generated by AI (Claude). If the bottle already had a description in the database it shows that; otherwise it generates one on demand and caches it. If generation fails, it tells you rather than silently showing nothing.
- **Flavor Profile** — clickable flavor tags. Clicking any tag takes you to Browse filtered to that flavor.
- **Community Rating** — average score across all user ratings
- **Community Reviews** — individual ratings from other users (username, stars, date, tasting notes). Updates immediately after you submit your own.
- **If you like this, try...** — 5 similar bottles chosen by a content-based recommendation engine (compares flavor profile, ABV, age, category)
- **Favorites button (♡/♥)** — save or unsave the bottle to your favorites list
- **Rate This Whiskey** — star picker (1–5) + optional tasting notes text field. Hit Submit to save. Your username is required.

### Flavor Wheel (`/flavor-wheel`)

An interactive SVG wheel showing the full taxonomy of whiskey flavors organized in three rings:

- **Inner ring** — broad flavor families (Fruity, Peated, Sweet, Spicy, etc.)
- **Middle ring** — subfamilies (e.g. "Dried Fruit", "Citrus" under Fruity)
- **Outer ring** — specific notes (e.g. "Fig", "Apricot", "Grapefruit")

**How to use it:**
- **Hover** any segment to see the flavor name and description in the side panel
- **Click** any segment to jump to Browse filtered to that flavor
- Use the **family shortcut chips** on the right to quickly filter by a whole flavor family

Good for when you know what flavors you like but aren't sure which category of whiskey to explore.

### Taste Quiz (`/quiz`)

A 5-step guided questionnaire that finds your starting point. Best for newcomers.

**The 5 questions:**
1. What flavors appeal to you? (pick multiple: sweet, fruity, spicy, vanilla, caramel, citrus, honey, oak, floral, nutty)
2. How smoky do you want it? (none / a little / heavily peated)
3. How bold? (light / medium / full-bodied)
4. What's your budget? (under $30 / $30–80 / $80–200 / $200+)
5. What style do you want to explore? (bourbon / scotch / irish / japanese / rye / surprise me)

**Results:** Top 6 personalized recommendations with a plain-English explanation of why each bottle matches your answers. Each result shows a match confidence score.

### For You (`/recommendations`)

Personalized recommendations based on whiskeys you've already rated.

**How to use it:**
1. Enter your username
2. Hit "Get Recommendations"
3. The engine looks at every bottle you've rated 3.5 stars or higher, builds a feature vector from your taste preferences, and returns the 8 most similar unrated bottles

The more you rate, the better this gets. Requires at least a few ratings to produce meaningful results.

---

### Value Picks (`/value-picks`)

Best bang-for-the-buck whiskeys ranked by a score of `rating ÷ log(price)` — a high community rating at a low price wins.

**Controls:**
- **Budget preset buttons** — Under $30 / Under $50 / Under $75 / Under $150. Click one to instantly apply it.
- **Category filter** — narrow to a specific style
- **Custom max price** — type any number to set an exact ceiling

Results are numbered (#1, #2, ...) so you can immediately see the ranking. Good for finding hidden gems or building a collection without overspending.

### Flights (`/flight-builder`)

A curated tasting flight is a guided progression of 3–4 bottles designed to teach your palate something specific. Each bottle in the flight comes with a "what to look for" lesson.

**Available themes:**

| Theme | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Beginner Flight | Approachable, sweet, smooth — all under $65. Best first flight. |
| 🔥 Smoky Journey | Progresses from barely smoky to full Islay peat bomb |
| 🥃 Bourbon Ladder | Entry → mid → premium → top shelf American whiskey |
| 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿 Scotch Regions | One bottle per Scottish region — Speyside, Highlands, Islay, Lowlands |
| 🌍 World Tour | One bottle per country — bourbon, scotch, irish, japanese, rye |
| 🌶️ Sweet to Spicy | From honeyed and soft to dry and fiery |
| ⏳ Age Progression | Same-style bottles at increasing age statements |

**How to use it:**
1. Click a theme card — the flight builds instantly
2. Optionally set a **max price per bottle** and click **Rebuild**
3. Work through the bottles in order — each has a step label and lesson telling you what to pay attention to

### Gift Finder (`/gift-finder`)

A 3–4 step wizard that recommends bottles as gifts.

**The questions:**
1. **Experience level** — Newcomer / Casual drinker / Enthusiast / Connoisseur
2. **Style they drink** — Bourbon / Scotch / Irish / Japanese / Rye / Not sure
3. **Your budget** — $25–55 / $55–110 / $110–220 / $220+
4. **Occasion** (optional) — Birthday / Holiday / Host gift / Just because

Clicking an option immediately advances to the next step. Hit "← Back" to change an answer. The final screen shows curated picks with a message explaining the selection. Hit "← Start Over" to run it again.

### My Palate (`/my-palate`)

A personal taste profile dashboard built from your ratings and favorites.

**What it shows:**
- **Stat cards** — total bottles rated, favorites saved, average rating you give, average price of bottles you engage with
- **Styles You Reach For** — bar chart of which categories (bourbon, scotch, etc.) you rate most
- **Flavor Fingerprint** — a word cloud + bar chart of the flavor notes most common in bottles you've rated highly
- **Your Ratings** — full history of every bottle you've rated with your score, tasting notes, and date
- **Saved Favorites** — all bottles you've hearted

This page reads your username from the browser. If you haven't rated anything yet, it prompts you to start. The more you use the app, the more meaningful your palate profile becomes.

---

### Compare (`/compare`)

Side-by-side comparison of any two bottles.

**How to use it:**
1. Type in the first search box — a dropdown appears as you type. Click a result to select it.
2. Type in the second search box and select the second bottle.
3. The two panels update immediately showing each bottle's full details with visual ABV and rating bars.
4. The **Head to Head** table below shows Price, Rating, Age, ABV, Category, and Region side by side. The winner in each metric is highlighted.

---

### Learn (`/learn`)

Educational content about whiskey. Contains:
- **Category guides** — in-depth explainers for each major style (bourbon, scotch, rye, irish, japanese, canadian) covering history, production, what to expect
- **Distillery stories** — profiles of notable producers
- **Glossary** — plain-English definitions for whiskey terms (single malt, NAS, cask strength, angel's share, etc.)

Good if you want to understand *why* whiskeys taste the way they do, not just which ones to buy.

### Ask SipSense (`/chat`)

A conversational AI assistant powered by a LangGraph agent backed by Claude. Has access to the full whiskey database and can do everything the other pages do, plus more.

**What you can ask it:**
- "What's a good peaty scotch under $60?"
- "What's the difference between bourbon and rye?"
- "Build me a beginner flight"
- "Find me something similar to Maker's Mark but smokier"
- "What makes Islay scotch different?"
- "I want to buy a gift for someone who likes Hibiki — what should I get?"
- "What's the best value bourbon right now?"
- "I like vanilla, caramel, and oak — what should I try?"

**How it works:**
- The agent has 23 tools it can call silently in the background (search, compare, find value picks, build flights, etc.) and combines them into a natural answer
- It **remembers your preferences** within and across sessions — if you say "I don't like peaty whiskey" it will remember that and factor it into future recommendations
- Inline **whiskey cards** appear when it surfaces specific bottles
- Use the starter prompts at the top if you're not sure where to begin
- **Shift+Enter** adds a newline; **Enter** sends the message
- **Start fresh** resets the conversation

## How the Recommendation Engine Works

SipSense uses **content-based filtering** — it builds a 27-dimensional feature vector for each bottle:

- 8 category flags (is it bourbon? scotch? etc.)
- 3 normalized numeric features (ABV, age, price)
- 16 flavor tag flags (smoky, vanilla, peaty, fruity, etc.)

When you rate whiskeys, it finds other bottles with high cosine similarity to the ones you liked. The **For You** page and the **Quiz** both use this engine. As you rate more bottles, the recommendations get more tuned to you.

## Tips

- **Set your username once** on any page — it's saved to your browser and used everywhere
- **Rate as you go** — even 5–10 ratings makes the For You page much more useful
- **Use the Flavor Wheel** if you know what you like flavor-wise but don't know what style to explore
- **Flight Builder + Beginner Flight** is the single best starting point if you're completely new
- **Value Picks under $50** is the best way to build a starter shelf without overpaying
- **Ask SipSense** anything — it's smarter than it looks and has access to every tool in the app
